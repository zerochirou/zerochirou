# Rhea

Rhea is a privacy-first, voice-based assistant designed exclusively for macOS. Unlike cloud-dependent alternatives, Rhea runs entirely locally on your machine, ensuring that your voice data and personal queries never leave your device.

## Local Intelligence

Leveraging the power of Apple Silicon, Rhea integrates optimized local inference engines. It utilizes CoreML and a local deployment of the Whisper model for rapid, highly accurate speech-to-text processing. Natural language understanding and task execution are handled by local LLMs via Ollama, enabling seamless OS-level integrations while maintaining absolute privacy and zero-latency performance.
