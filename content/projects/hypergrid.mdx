# Hypergrid

Hypergrid is an advanced analytical instrument designed to evaluate road infrastructure capabilities. By combining big data processing with deep learning models, it provides urban planners and engineers with actionable insights into traffic patterns, structural integrity, and future infrastructure requirements.

## Architecture & Data Pipeline

The platform ingests massive datasets from various IoT sensors and traffic cameras. This data is processed through our proprietary deep learning models built with PyTorch, which identify anomalies and predict infrastructure strain. The insights are then visualized through a high-performance Next.js dashboard, enabling real-time decision-making.
